SOLARIS | HACKCBS 2026
DATA FLOW DIAGRAMS (DFD) AND DATA DICTIONARY
Version: 2.0 | Updated: 27 October 2026 | Scope: ten-feature HackCBS MVP
Source: team-provided Solaris feature list, F01-F10. The What-If Simulator belongs to F05.

NOTATION
[E#] = external entity (actor or externally issued source document)
(P#) = logical process
||D#|| = persistent data store
--> = data flow
These are logical information flows, NOT electrical energy flows. DISCOM-issued documents are imported by authorised users; Solaris does not operate an official billing/approval connection.

LEVEL 0 | SYSTEM CONTEXT
[E1 Household resident] -- registration, self-reported limits, monthly kWh, consent, pre-/post-install bills --> (P0 SOLARIS)
[E2 Society administrator] -- community/roof/project details, readiness evidence, accepted plan, approval evidence --> (P0 SOLARIS)
[E3 Solar provider] -- quotation, equipment, milestones, generation/eligible-export/settlement CSV --> (P0 SOLARIS)
[E4 External DISCOM/authority records] -- rules, official approvals, official credits/settlement evidence (uploaded by E1/E2/E3) --> (P0 SOLARIS)
(P0 SOLARIS) -- resident plan comparisons, projected benefits, personal financial dashboards, reconciliation reports --> [E1]
(P0 SOLARIS) -- readiness status, aggregate feasibility/optimiser, accepted terms, exception summaries --> [E2]
(P0 SOLARIS) -- assigned project scope, status and cost-version feedback --> [E3]
NOTE: Outputs are informational. No official approval is granted, no official bill is issued, and no credit is transferred through Solaris.

CROSS-PROCESS FEEDBACK
(P7 provider updated quotation) --> (P4 feasibility recalculation) --> (P5 updated contribution plans); accepted/approved plan versions remain distinguishable from newly estimated versions.
(P9 confirmed official credits) --> (P8 current household financial record) and (P10 realised-vs-planned results).
(P5 What-If) reads a frozen baseline snapshot and creates a separate scenario in ||D6||; it does not mutate an accepted plan in ||D7||.

LEVEL 2A | F02 READINESS (NONBINDING)
(P2.1) Read community location, DISCOM, consumer/connection type and proposed meter/project configuration from ||D1/D3||.
(P2.2) Retrieve relevant dated requirements from ||D2||; mark missing or unreviewed sources.
(P2.3) Check known site/roof and account prerequisites; list missing evidence and potential blockers.
(P2.4) Return NEEDS_VERIFICATION / REQUIREMENTS_NOTED / POTENTIAL_ISSUE as informational assessment.
(P2.5) Record actual externally supplied DISCOM approval evidence separately, with issuer, date and reference; mark demo sample as synthetic.
(P2.6) Prevent downstream records from claiming official approval without accepted external evidence.

LEVEL 2B | F03 PRE-INSTALL INPUT + OCR
(P3.1) Collect household monthly consumption and maximum upfront/monthly financing limits; optional bill amount and history.
(P3.2) Receive optional PDF/image and produce tentative OCR bill fields.
(P3.3) Resident reviews and corrects all relevant fields; save consent/source and confirmation state.
(P3.4) Validate time periods, kWh units, duplicate periods and budget ceilings.
(P3.5) Write clean inputs to ||D3|| and provide them to (P4)/(P5).

LEVEL 2C | F04/F05 SOLAR FEASIBILITY AND BENEFIT-AWARE OPTIMISATION
(P4.1) Calculate candidate PV size from roof/yield assumptions; model monthly/annual generation.
(P4.2) Apply quoted/projected cost, financing and maintenance assumptions.
(P4.3) Apply explicitly selected hypothetical eligible-export/settlement, allocation and carry-forward assumptions to estimate household financial outcomes.
(P4.4) Store dated and versioned projection in ||D5||.
(P5.1) Snapshot available project and household data and contribution ceilings.
(P5.2) Build equal, affordability-aware and benefit-aware plan candidates with financing and ownership-share constraints.
(P5.3) Validate funding coverage, share totals, household caps and applicable credit allocation restrictions; report solver status and exclusions.
(P5.4) Store plans, monthly payments, projected credit-value ranges, net financial benefits and payback in ||D6||.
(P5.5) For What-If, clone baseline, modify withdrawal/cost/yield assumptions, rerun and save a distinct scenario in ||D6||.

LEVEL 2D | F06/F07 AGREEMENTS AND PROVIDER DATA
(P6.1) Resident explicitly accepts/declines a contribution plan; store consent and plan version.
(P6.2) Admin records community acceptance and proposed shares.
(P6.3) Import source evidence for actual DISCOM approval; store active allocation version and effective date only with appropriate evidence.
(P7.1) Provider uploads quote and specifications; new quote version triggers F04/F05 stale-input warning.
(P7.2) Provider records commissioning and equipment milestones.
(P7.3) Provider imports generated energy, exported energy and settlement records separately, each with date and source.

LEVEL 2E | F09 CREDIT RECONCILIATION
(P9.1) Receive post-install official bill from household (PDF/image) with preliminary OCR fields.
(P9.2) Require household confirmation of billing dates, credit kWh/INR and relevant consumption fields; save corrected source.
(P9.3) Retrieve applicable approved allocation version, settlement evidence, opening balances and policy.
(P9.4) Match periods and accounting units; if insufficient, mark UNVERIFIABLE rather than invent expected credit.
(P9.5) Compute justified expected credit for matched period; compare against recorded official credit and carry-forward/settlement treatment.
(P9.6) Check common explanations: date mismatch, share revision, carried-forward credit, OCR correction and supported settlement adjustment.
(P9.7) Emit EXPLAINED / UNRESOLVED / UNVERIFIABLE case and source-linked discrepancy report into ||D8||.

LEVEL 2F | F08/F10 FINANCIAL LEDGER AND PLAN-TO-REALITY
(P8.1) Retrieve accepted ownership/contribution and dated payment obligations.
(P8.2) Read official recorded benefit, financing instalments, maintenance and other recorded project costs.
(P8.3) Generate monthly household cash-flow line items, cumulative values, current payback context and INTERNAL statement into ||D10||.
(P10.1) Retrieve original locked F04/F05 projection from ||D5||.
(P10.2) Align projected and actual months, identify changed price/cost/yield/usage where evidence supports them.
(P10.3) Recompute net-benefit variance and indicative future payback without rewriting initial forecast.
(P10.4) Produce explanatory report with VERIFIED / POSSIBLE / UNKNOWN causes into ||D11||.

DATA STORES / ACCESS
D1: identities, roles, community membership, invitations and consent [tenant + user scoped].
D2: dated policy references, nonbinding readiness results and external approval evidence [audited].
D3: private monthly consumption, self-reported upfront/monthly ceilings, optional pre-install bills and OCR confirmations [resident scoped].
D4: provider quotes, versions, equipment, commissioning, generated/exported/settlement import files [assigned provider + authorised admin].
D5: feasibility assumptions, tariff/credit model, frozen projection versions, quote/evidence references [versioned].
D6: candidate plan runs, selected households, proposed ownership/credit shares, embedded what-if results [restricted project].
D7: accepted agreements, dated ownership and approved allocation versions, authority evidence [restricted, audited].
D8: confirmed post-install household bills, applied/carried-forward credit ledger, mismatch causes/reports [resident scoped; admin minimum necessary].
D9: household/provider project financing, maintenance and payment records [restricted].
D10: monthly current-finance statements and cash-flow history [resident scoped].
D11: original-vs-actual variance reports and updated payback [resident scoped].
D12: tamper-evident audit/source identifiers, document provenance, scenario version links [authorised audit scope].

DATA DICTIONARY / CRITICAL FIELDS
monthly_consumption_kwh: self-reported or reviewed bill units, with billing period and source.
max_upfront_inr: resident-reported upper bound, not committed payment.
max_monthly_financing_inr: resident-reported upper bound on loan instalments, not Solaris subscription.
accepted_upfront_inr / accepted_financed_amount_inr: specific approved household participation choice.
ownership_share_fraction: fractional ownership under agreed terms.
credit_allocation_share_fraction: proposed or officially approved VNM credit allocation; effective date and status required.
generation_kwh: gross PV electricity output; never implicitly eligible for official credits.
eligible_settlement_kwh: approved eligible credit basis if evidence exists.
expected_credit_kwh: calculated from approved allocation and suitable settlement evidence; otherwise unavailable.
recorded_bill_credit_kwh / recorded_bill_credit_inr: figures taken from confirmed official utility bill or settlement record.
credit_carry_forward_kwh: separately documented surplus carried into another eligible period.
credit_applied_kwh: portion used in the current relevant period.
net_financial_benefit_inr: credit monetary benefit less allocable loan payments, maintenance and other operating cash costs for a defined period; capital contribution accounted at its actual date for cumulative cash flow.
original_projection_id: immutable pre-operation financial assumptions for F10 comparison.
credit_reconciliation_state: EXPLAINED, UNRESOLVED or UNVERIFIABLE; evidence references mandatory for any verified claim.

TRUST / PRIVACY BOUNDARIES
TB1 Browser -> API: authenticated requests and server-verified project/household permissions.
TB2 Resident -> Society admin: private bill/budget not disclosed by default; coordinator receives only necessary aggregate information.
TB3 Provider -> Society: quotes/asset files visible only for assigned project, not private participant records.
TB4 Uploaded DISCOM-looking docs -> 'official evidence': file authenticity and applicability require review; synthetic files visibly tagged as demo.
TB5 Model calculations -> official records: estimated output cannot be promoted to 'DISCOM confirmed' without the actual source.
TB6 Pre-project financial projections -> post-install performance: preserve old versions for audit and fair comparison.

ERROR / EXCEPTION FLOWS
Missing consent -> reject access or processing requiring that consent.
Missing policy proof -> readiness NEEDS_VERIFICATION, never automatic 'eligible/approved'.
Invalid or rejected OCR -> require correction; exclude unconfirmed field from authoritative calculations.
Quote change -> mark feasibility and optimizer results stale until recomputed.
Optimiser infeasible -> explain budget/funding/roof/share constraints; propose alternative model only as hypothetical.
Settlement missing or unmatched -> reconciliation UNVERIFIABLE; show source/period needs.
Unused credits -> track carry-forward/settlement; do not report as missing merely because unused in one bill.
Incorrect official billing value suspected -> report possible mismatch with evidence, do not accuse DISCOM.
Cumulative finance mismatch -> block statement publication and investigate double-counting.
