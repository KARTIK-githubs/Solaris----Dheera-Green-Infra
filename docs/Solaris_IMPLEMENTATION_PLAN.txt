SOLARIS | HACKCBS 2026
IMPLEMENTATION PLAN AND DELIVERY ROADMAP
Version: 2.0 | Updated: 27 October 2026 | Scope: ten-feature HackCBS MVP
Reference: team-provided Solaris final feature list F01-F10 (What-If integrated with F05).

ASSUMPTIONS
One reproducible synthetic residential community (illustratively 20 separately metered households), one clearly labelled modelled VNM arrangement, no live DISCOM/inverter integration. An external provider quotation and approval/settlement records may be simulated but MUST be labelled synthetic. Tech suggestion: React/TypeScript frontend + FastAPI backend + SQLite + manual/CSV import + reviewed OCR. Timeline below is a hackathon cutdown, not a production delivery guarantee.

TEAM WORKSTREAMS (ONE PERSON MAY COVER MULTIPLE)
PR: product/regulatory assumptions and documentation, synthetic bill and settlement evidence, demo accuracy.
FE: role-aware frontend, household onboarding, plan comparison, resident and provider dashboards.
BE: project/member data, auth/consent, document/OCR review, import and report APIs.
OPT: capacity/finance maths, optimiser, what-if, ledger accounting, reconciliation tests.
UX/QA: copy, source states, accessibility, end-to-end tests, demo script.

PHASE 0 | ALIGN SCOPE AND TEST DATA
- Confirm ten features, their boundaries, and integration: F05 includes What-If; F03 and F09 reuse bill OCR; F08 is current finances while F10 is projection-versus-reality.
- Choose a single hypothetical community, DISCOM/rule assumption set and roof/site size. Mark unknown rules as needing verification.
- Prepare ~20 synthetic household profiles (monthly kWh, upfront/monthly maxima), optional sample historic bills, one provider quote and basic yield/cost assumptions.
- Prepare separate synthetic post-install official bills and settlement CSVs, including matching records, a period mismatch, a carried-forward credit and an unexplained mismatch.
- Define a documented unit convention, dates, version identifiers and accounting rules to prevent credit and financing double counting.
EXIT: signed-off feature mapping, data fixtures, user roles and mock policy assumptions.

PHASE 1 | CORE ONBOARDING (F01-F04)
FE: project creation, invitations, resident account screen, site form, simple readiness checklist, consumption/budget form and optional PDF/image upload.
BE: tenancy/RBAC, project and household schema, file upload, extraction/review state and consent log.
PR: source/date-backed readiness questions, 'requires official approval' copy and example missing-document cases.
OPT: deterministic capacity/yield and cost calculation, with generation != eligible settlement rule separation.
TEST: one resident cannot see another resident's private data; manual inputs work without OCR; OCR requires confirmation.
EXIT: coordinator can form the society and generate a labelled preliminary feasibility estimate.

PHASE 2 | CONTRIBUTION PLANNING (F05-F06)
OPT: equal-contribution baseline; affordability-constrained plan; benefit-aware objective under explicit assumptions; show shares, payments, estimated benefits, net returns and payback.
FE: three-plan comparison, per-household explanation and embedded what-if scenario controls.
BE: immutable scenario snapshot, proposal acceptance, ownership/share register, consent and external-approval evidence states.
TEST: budgets respected, project financing accounted for, ownership shares reconcile, solver infeasibility explained and baseline survives what-if edits.
EXIT: a 20-household demo can meaningfully compare plans and accept a proposal without claiming DISCOM approval.

PHASE 3 | PROVIDER AND OPERATIONAL EVIDENCE (F07)
FE: scoped solar-provider project workspace, quote upload and milestones.
BE: quote versioning, commissioning/approval evidence and manual/CSV generation, export and settlement uploads.
OPT: recalculate F04/F05 when quote changes; distinguish gross generation from credit-eligible quantities.
TEST: provider cannot access private household bills or budgets; quote revision flags stale plan; unsupported settlement cannot be verified.
EXIT: approved/synthetic project records can feed the post-installation workflow with visible provenance.

PHASE 4 | BENEFITS, RECONCILIATION AND PLAN-TO-REALITY (F08-F10)
BE: reusable bill OCR review for post-installation bills; period-aligned settlement ledger; financial cost ledger; personal statements and evidence reports.
OPT: expected vs official credits under accepted rule/data, carry-forward treatment, financial benefit calculation, original projection snapshot vs actual variance and updated indicative payback.
FE: household dashboard, bill upload/correction, discrepancy cases, projection-versus-actual comparison and labels for UNVERIFIABLE state.
TEST: no double credit, no principal double count, mismatched periods aren't incorrectly flagged, corrected OCR drives reconciliation, original projected snapshot retained.
EXIT: household can see own realised benefits, upload official-bill evidence, investigate a mismatch and compare actual outcomes against the plan.

HACKCBS INTEGRATION PRIORITY (IF TIME BECOMES TIGHT)
P0: cohesive demonstration of F01-F05 plus minimal F06/F07 source records and F09 comparison against clearly labelled synthetic settlement and post-install bills.
P1: polished F08 household financial history and F10 projection-vs-actual; explainable discrepancy export, edge cases and scenario comparison UI.
P2: broader OCR bill layouts, extended policy bibliography, comprehensive user administration and export formatting.
Keep the ten-feature product model coherent even if some surfaces are simplified; avoid fake success states, guessed credits or unsupported regulatory conclusions.

DEMO STORY / CLICK ORDER
1. Society administrator creates a 20-household project with roles (F01).
2. Enters DISCOM/site and sees a nonbinding VNM requirements checklist (F02).
3. Residents enter monthly consumption and upfront/monthly financing limits; one uploads a bill and corrects OCR (F03).
4. Calculator returns capacity, yield, cost and indicative financial value (F04).
5. Run equal, affordability-aware and benefit-aware plans; then remove a member or reduce solar yield in What-If (F05).
6. Record resident acceptance and show draft vs externally approved allocation status (F06).
7. Provider submits a quote that revises the plan, logs commissioning and imports synthetic settlement data (F07).
8. Resident sees credits, monthly cash flow and investment recovery (F08).
9. Resident uploads a post-installation bill, confirms OCR and views a matched credit comparison or an explainable unresolved/unverifiable state (F09).
10. Compare original projected vs actual bill benefits, net cash flow and revised projected payback (F10).

RELEASE QUALITY CHECKLIST
[ ] All core screens use the same contribution, share, unit and settlement definitions.
[ ] Every estimated vs verified credit state is labelled and backed by document or assumption.
[ ] Monthly loan principal is not counted again as an additional project investment.
[ ] Bill credits already netted in utility amounts are not subtracted twice.
[ ] Credits carried forward are distinguished from missing credits.
[ ] Current provider quotation and accepted plan versions are aligned or explicitly shown as stale.
[ ] Missing official settlement prevents a definitive verified credit comparison.
[ ] RWA/provider role cannot access unrelated private resident files.
[ ] OCR fields must be accepted/corrected by residents before use.
[ ] Inputs and original projected values are frozen for meaningful later-versus-actual comparison.
[ ] Demo is reproducible on documented synthetic records with appropriate disclaimers.

RISKS AND CONTINGENCIES
Unverified VNM rules -> readiness only, modelled financial outcomes explicitly hypothetical.
Missing settlement evidence -> credit verification unavailable; don't claim 70 vs 55 is an actual undercredit.
Solver infeasible -> show explanation and candidate alternatives, not fabricated plan.
OCR unreliable -> use manual confirmation and ready sample bills.
Insufficient time -> prioritise one fully connected 20-household pathway over unused dashboards or external integrations.
