SOLARIS | HACKCBS 2026
PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)
Version: 2.0 | Updated: 27 October 2026 | Status: proposed HackCBS MVP specification
Source of truth: "Solaris — Final HackCBS Feature List" supplied by the team (10 numbered features).

IMPORTANT BOUNDARY
Solaris is a planning, coordination and financial-verification platform for shared solar projects. It is NOT a DISCOM, electricity retailer, financing provider, installer, legal adviser, official utility-bill issuer or VNM approval authority. Demo data and unsupported regulatory assumptions must be labelled. The actual availability, eligibility and credit treatment of VNM require jurisdiction-specific confirmation.

1. PRODUCT VISION
Help housing societies plan shared solar participation that is financially feasible and potentially worthwhile for individual households, coordinate agreed ownership and installation, and verify the financial outcomes of the project.

2. PROBLEM / OPPORTUNITY
- Households have different electricity demand, upfront budgets and capacity for monthly financing payments.
- A contribution plan that fits the budgets of residents is not necessarily a financially worthwhile plan for each participant.
- Society coordinators must distinguish proposed ownership and credit shares from approved arrangements.
- The records needed after commissioning are fragmented across provider generation files, settlement evidence, official household bills and expense records.
- A correct official credit allocation does not necessarily mean the project's actual returns match its original projections.

3. TARGET USERS AND JOBS TO BE DONE
P1 Resident/participant: provide electricity and budget inputs; compare proposals; accept or decline an arrangement; see own contributions, credits, costs and progress toward payback.
P2 Community administrator / RWA coordinator: create a project; invite members; perform preliminary readiness checks; compare plans; record consents and approvals; review reconciliation exceptions and community-level progress.
P3 Solar provider: submit quotation and specifications; update installation and commissioning milestones; upload available generation, export and settlement records with project-scoped access.
P4 Platform administrator (limited): manage configurations, permissions and supporting records without routinely accessing residents' private bills or budgets.

4. PRODUCT GOALS
G1 Explainable community registration, household input collection and preliminary solar/VNM assessment.
G2 Produce comparable equal-contribution, affordability-aware and benefit-aware participation plans under explicit budgets, financial assumptions and applicable credit-share constraints.
G3 Present each household's expected benefits and financial downside, including the integrated what-if simulator.
G4 Record accepted contributions, ownership, consent, proposed allocations and official approval evidence as separate states.
G5 Trace official bills and settlement evidence to verified credits or clearly mark reconciliation as unavailable.
G6 Show households' actual financial position and compare realised benefits against dated pre-installation projections.
G7 Provide a realistic, reproducible HackCBS demonstration without pretending to have live DISCOM or inverter integrations.

NON-GOALS
- Granting or predicting official VNM approval with certainty.
- Generating official electricity bills or transferring/reallocating credits directly.
- Selling energy, peer-to-peer electricity trading or dispatching energy.
- Issuing loans, charging residents through payment processing, signing legal contracts or guaranteeing returns.
- Standalone MSME onboarding, technician ticketing, maintenance marketplace or real-time inverter telemetry.

5. FEATURE SCOPE (TEN FEATURES)

A. COMMUNITY ONBOARDING AND FEASIBILITY
F01 Community Registration and Role-Based Access
Purpose: create a housing society project and invite residents; give residents, administrators and providers separate access to relevant records.
Inputs: society details, invitation/member data, roles and permissions.
Output: project membership, individual and community views, authorised provider workspace.
Safety: resident bill and budget information private by default; enforce project/role boundaries.

F02 VNM Readiness Assessment
Purpose: inform the society about potentially applicable rules and outstanding conditions before an investment decision.
Inputs: location, DISCOM, consumer/connection type, proposed metering model, site and roof details.
Output: preliminary readiness checklist; potential issues; missing evidence; externally granted approval status.
Safety: show requirement sources/dates when available; never label the project 'DISCOM-approved' without actual evidence.

F03 Household Electricity and Financial Data Collection
Purpose: capture demand and household-imposed financial limits.
Inputs: average monthly kWh (optional monthly history); maximum upfront contribution; maximum affordable monthly financing instalment; optional bill amount; optional pre-installation PDF/image bills.
Output: validated household profiles; reviewed OCR fields; input completeness and provenance.
Safety: OCR errors corrected by resident; maximum budgets are self-reported limits, not accepted payment pledges or assessed creditworthiness.

F04 Solar Feasibility Calculator (Onboarding)
Purpose: establish indicative rooftop solar capacity, installation costs and expected generation and financial outcomes.
Inputs: rooftop area/rights, location, demand, yield/cost assumptions, and available provider quotes.
Output: feasible candidate capacity and indicative monthly/annual generation, cost and payback ranges.
Safety: clearly distinguish generation from eligible export/settlement and financial bill credits; estimates are not technical approvals or guarantees. Update calculations when a provider's actual quote arrives.

B. INVESTMENT AND OWNERSHIP PLANNING
F05 Benefit-Aware Contribution and Ownership Optimiser (USP 1)
Purpose: propose participation plans that satisfy available household budgets and project requirements while evaluating projected net financial benefit per household.
Inputs: limits, consumption, feasible capacity/cost, financing terms, maintenance, generation, permissible ownership/credit-share model and settlement assumptions.
Output: compare equal, affordability-aware and benefit-aware plans; proposed upfront/monthly payments; ownership and projected credit shares; net benefit/payback; explicit infeasibility/exclusion reasons.
Integrated What-If Simulator: recalculate scenarios for withdrawals, increased costs and lower generation; identify households whose financial conditions no longer hold.
Safety: member selection is not proof of benefit; projected credit shares must follow applicable approved arrangements; no arbitrary redistribution of official credits; user reviews and accepts any contribution proposal.

F06 Contribution, Ownership and Agreement Records
Purpose: store what participants actually accept and what the authorities actually approve.
Inputs: agreed contribution/ownership, resident consent, draft and approved allocation policies, agreement and approval documents.
Output: per-resident and community summary with time/version tracking, separate proposed/member-accepted/DISCOM-approved states.
Safety: application consent is not legal execution and proposed allocation is not an authorised DISCOM allocation.

C. SOLAR PROVIDER AND PROJECT MANAGEMENT
F07 Solar Provider Dashboard (Company Side)
Purpose: receive installer quotes and track installation, commissioning, generation and available settlement records.
Inputs: quotes, equipment/site specifications, milestones, commissioning and approval evidence, generation/export/settlement files.
Output: provider project view, status records and manual/CSV imports; confirmed quotes feed back into F04/F05.
Safety: providers see project-specific authorised information, not residents' private financial profiles; generation is not automatically creditable export.

D. FINANCIAL MONITORING AND VERIFICATION
F08 Household Financial Benefits Dashboard
Purpose: give a resident an intelligible view of their actual position over time.
Inputs: accepted contributions, officially recorded credits, financing instalments, maintenance costs and historical records.
Output: own contribution/share, monetary bill-credit benefits, expenses, monthly net benefits, cumulative cash-flow view, simple and cash-flow-aware payback context, monthly internal statement.
Safety: no double subtraction of credit already incorporated in a net bill, or double counting of financing principal as an additional investment; internal statements are not DISCOM bills.

F09 Solar Credit Reconciliation and Discrepancy Investigation (USP 2)
Purpose: check approved expected credits against official recorded credits for matching settlement/billing periods.
Inputs: resident's post-installation PDF/image bill, reviewed OCR fields, approved allocation version, eligible settlement/export evidence, applicable billing rules.
Output: expected-versus-recorded credit comparison, period and carry-forward checks, possible causes, unresolved discrepancy report or clear 'unverifiable' state.
Safety: cannot infer official credits from gross PV generation; investigate period, carry-forward, allocation and OCR differences before claiming an error; never assert utility fault without sufficient evidence.

F10 Projected vs Actual Financial Benefit Tracking (USP 3)
Purpose: compare original pre-installation financial projections against actual realised credits and costs.
Inputs: frozen versioned F04/F05 projections, actual generation and settlement, F09 confirmed credits, financing/O&M data and changes in consumption/assumptions.
Output: planned-vs-actual credit value, expenses, net benefit, variance explanations, updated projected payback and household-level performance summary.
Safety: indicate which variance causes are demonstrated by evidence and which are hypotheses; preserve original prediction rather than silently rewriting history.

6. USER JOURNEYS
J1 Community administrator: create project -> invite households -> input site and regulatory details -> perform F02 readiness assessment -> obtain F03 data -> run F04 -> compare F05 plans and stress tests -> record F06 acceptance -> hand off relevant scope to provider.
J2 Resident before installation: join -> provide manual kWh/budget or reviewed OCR data -> review proposed contribution, ownership, projected benefits and downside -> explicitly consent/accept or decline.
J3 Provider: accept assigned access -> upload quotation -> coordinator reruns F04/F05 -> track external approvals, installation and commissioning -> upload generation/export/settlement records.
J4 Resident after installation: upload post-installation bill and verify OCR -> view own official recorded credits and net financial position -> investigate F09 discrepancies -> view F10 projection-versus-actual tracking.

7. EXPERIENCE / SCREEN MAP
Landing/project join; project onboarding; society and DISCOM readiness checklist; resident inputs and reviewed bill OCR; feasibility estimates; optimiser three-plan comparison and embedded what-if; proposal acceptance and approval register; scoped provider workspace; resident finance dashboard; post-installation bill upload and reconciliation; plan-to-reality tracking and internal report.

8. HACKCBS DEMONSTRATION
Use a clearly labelled synthetic housing society (for example 20 Delhi households) with differentiated consumption and contribution ceilings. Show: project creation -> readiness flagged as preliminary -> bill OCR with manual correction -> capacity and illustrative cost estimate -> equal vs affordability vs benefit-aware comparison -> withdrawal / lower-generation what-if -> draft acceptance -> provider quotation re-prices the plan -> simulated approval/commissioning records -> import synthetic eligible settlement and household post-installation bill -> detect and explain credit mismatch or mark records insufficient -> show planned vs realised benefit.
Every simulated approval or official record must be explicitly labelled as synthetic evidence.

9. SUCCESS CRITERIA
- A 20-household dataset produces consistent, reproducible projections and plan comparisons with explicit solver statuses.
- No plan violates upfront/monthly ceilings, allocation rules, capacity constraints or funding balance; infeasible cases are explained.
- Proposed credit shares and authoritative credits are never conflated.
- Resident OCR corrections are reflected in calculations with provenance.
- Mismatched or missing settlement periods do not produce falsely verified discrepancies.
- A credit already included in a billed amount is not subtracted again; project investment/financing costs are not double-counted.
- Plan-to-reality report preserves original projections and attributes only evidence-supported differences.
- Users from one project cannot access another household's private files or budgets.

10. OPEN DECISIONS FOR THE TEAM
- Which dated Delhi/DISCOM regulatory sources and metering configuration will be used for the mock project?
- Which synthetic tariff, export/settlement and credit carry-forward assumptions are used, and where are they displayed?
- What happens if a household cannot meet a minimum financial-return target? Exclude, flag or offer alternatives with explicit choice?
- Which financing choices and monthly repayment assumptions are actually simulated?
- Which provider quote, actual bill formats and synthetic settlement CSV schema are supported?
- What fields are necessary for reliably distinguishing expected credits, used credits and carried-forward balances?

11. COMMERCIAL HYPOTHESIS (NOT MVP FUNCTIONALITY)
Potential software customer: housing society managers or solar providers. Subscription or per-project pricing is unvalidated and is not a required resident payment in this demo.
