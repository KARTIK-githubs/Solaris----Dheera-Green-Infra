SOLARIS | HACKCBS 2026
SOFTWARE REQUIREMENTS SPECIFICATION (SRS)
Version: 2.0 | Updated: 27 October 2026 | Status: HackCBS MVP requirement baseline
Reference: team-provided Solaris final feature list, F01-F10; F05 includes What-If Simulator.

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
Build a multi-role shared-solar planning and financial verification web application for residential communities, their households and assigned solar providers. It must support preliminary VNM readiness, self-reported financial input, solar feasibility, benefit-aware participation planning, agreement/approval records, provider data capture, household financial views, credit reconciliation and projection-to-actual tracking. These are decision support functions, not official utility actions.

2. ACTORS / ACCESS
R01 Resident: enter/correct own monthly consumption, budgets and electricity bills; review proposed plan and consent; see only own detailed financial and reconciliation records.
R02 Society administrator: create project, invite members, enter roof/rule assumptions, compare aggregate plans, manage consent and approval records, access project exceptions without default access to raw resident bill details.
R03 Solar provider: see assigned projects; submit quotes, equipment, milestones, generation/export/settlement records; no access to individual household budget or bill by default.
R04 System administrator: manage configuration and limited support with audit logs and least-privilege permissions.

3. FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

F01 COMMUNITY REGISTRATION AND ROLE-BASED ACCESS
FR-001 MUST: Allow society project creation with community, location and DISCOM fields.
FR-002 MUST: Invite/register households and link users to a project with consent-aware membership status.
FR-003 MUST: Enforce resident, administrator and provider roles on server endpoints and file access.
FR-004 MUST: Provide an appropriate project overview per role without leaking cross-household financial data.

F02 VNM READINESS ASSESSMENT
FR-005 MUST: Collect location, DISCOM, connection type/metering model, proposed project type and relevant roof/site details.
FR-006 MUST: Display known requirements with source/date when supplied, and identify missing/possibly conflicting evidence.
FR-007 MUST: Return a nonbinding readiness state, such as REQUIREMENTS_NOTED / POTENTIAL_ISSUE / NEEDS_VERIFICATION / APPROVAL_RECORDED; never equate readiness with DISCOM approval.
FR-008 MUST: Record official DISCOM approval status only against an associated externally supplied evidence item.
FR-009 MUST: Refuse to present unsupported VNM credit allocation as officially approved.

F03 HOUSEHOLD ELECTRICITY AND FINANCIAL DATA COLLECTION
FR-010 MUST: Accept average monthly consumption (kWh), optional historical months, maximum upfront contribution and maximum affordable monthly financing instalment.
FR-011 MUST: Make pre-installation bill amount and PDF/image upload optional; allow manual entry without documents.
FR-012 MUST: Extract bill fields via OCR in the MVP when uploaded and require resident review/confirmation before calculations use them.
FR-013 MUST: Track source, uploaded document, user corrections, billing period and confirmation state where applicable.
FR-014 MUST: Validate units, invalid/negative amounts, duplicate months, dates and missing required fields.
FR-015 MUST: Treat household limits as upper bounds, not accepted amounts; require explicit acceptance under F06.

F04 SOLAR FEASIBILITY CALCULATOR
FR-016 MUST: Model candidate installation capacity from usable roof, location, generation-yield and technical assumptions, with household demand context.
FR-017 MUST: Estimate cost, monthly/annual generation and indicative financial outcomes with transparent assumptions.
FR-018 MUST: Distinguish PV generation, eligible export/settlement, modelled household credit (kWh) and monetary bill benefit (INR).
FR-019 MUST: Refresh feasible scenarios and alert affected users when a confirmed provider quote changes the cost basis.
FR-020 MUST: Mark results as indicative and dependent on technical/regulatory verification.

F05 BENEFIT-AWARE CONTRIBUTION AND OWNERSHIP OPTIMISER (USP 1)
FR-021 MUST: Accept household upfront/monthly ceilings, project funding requirement, consent, financing assumptions, capacity, maintenance and approved/permissible share constraints.
FR-022 MUST: Generate and compare equal-contribution, affordability-aware and benefit-aware candidate plans when feasible.
FR-023 MUST: Calculate proposed contribution, financing schedule, ownership share, estimated eligible credit allocation, carry-forward/settlement treatment, net benefit and payback assumptions per participating household.
FR-024 MUST: Do not allocate official credits arbitrarily; use the eligible ownership-to-allocation relation for the selected project model, and label hypothetical shares as proposed.
FR-025 MUST: Show all unmet constraints, excluded/unaccommodated households, solver feasibility and objective; do not claim mathematical optimality without proof.
FR-026 MUST: Allow a household to accept or reject a proposed contribution explicitly through F06.
FR-027 MUST: Embed What-If simulations for household withdrawal, quote/cost increase and lower generation; recalculate funding, per-household benefit and downside flags without overwriting the baseline plan.
FR-028 MUST: Present scenario comparisons without assuming larger investments always produce superior rates of return.

F06 CONTRIBUTION, OWNERSHIP AND AGREEMENT RECORDS
FR-029 MUST: Store draft contributions, ownership and allocation proposals separately from participant-accepted terms and DISCOM-approved evidence.
FR-030 MUST: Record consent, acceptance time, user, agreement version and supporting documents.
FR-031 MUST: Ensure active ownership/credit shares sum to the applicable total; document rounding and effective dates.
FR-032 MUST: Preserve historical versions when shares, participants, quotes or approvals change.

F07 SOLAR PROVIDER DASHBOARD
FR-033 MUST: Enable assigned providers to upload quotation, equipment/site details and project milestones.
FR-034 MUST: Record commissioning and externally granted approval status/evidence without manufacturing official approval.
FR-035 MUST: Accept generation, eligible export and settlement records through manual entry or CSV import, each with explicit source, period and unit.
FR-036 MUST: Separate generation from export and settlement. Provider records alone are not automatically verified official DISCOM data.
FR-037 MUST: Trigger review or recalculation when the quote changes assumptions used by F04/F05.

F08 HOUSEHOLD FINANCIAL BENEFITS DASHBOARD
FR-038 MUST: Display own accepted contributions, ownership shares, credits on official bills, project expenses and payment timelines.
FR-039 MUST: Calculate monetary bill-credit benefit, monthly net benefit, cumulative cash flow and investment recovery using consistent accounting rules.
FR-040 MUST: Display internal monthly statements clearly marked 'NOT AN OFFICIAL UTILITY BILL'.
FR-041 MUST: Separate observed/confirmed figures from hypothetical/projection figures and identify their sources.
FR-042 MUST: Prevent double-counting of financing principal against upfront investment and double deduction of an already-netted credit on a utility bill.

F09 SOLAR CREDIT RECONCILIATION AND DISCREPANCY INVESTIGATION (USP 2)
FR-043 MUST: Let residents upload post-installation official utility bills (PDF/image) and confirm OCR-extracted credit, meter, billing-period and relevant consumption fields.
FR-044 MUST: Read a dated approved allocation version and suitable period-matched official settlement/eligible export data before presenting a 'verified expected credit'.
FR-045 MUST: Compare expected and recorded credits only after checking unit, billing period, settlement timing and adjustments.
FR-046 MUST: Track credit carried-forward, applied and settled separately when supporting evidence exists; do not treat carried-forward credits as inherently missing.
FR-047 MUST: Identify potential causes: period mismatch, allocation revision, carry-forward/settlement adjustment, OCR error and unresolved discrepancy.
FR-048 MUST: Produce a resident/project-scoped evidence report for unresolved discrepancies; label unknown causes as hypotheses, not conclusions.
FR-049 MUST: Show UNVERIFIABLE when approved rules or adequate settlement evidence are absent, even if generation data is available.

F10 PROJECTED VS ACTUAL FINANCIAL BENEFIT TRACKING (USP 3)
FR-050 MUST: Freeze/date the original version of household projections from F04/F05 before operational records are compared.
FR-051 MUST: Compare projected and recorded credit value, project costs and net benefits over aligned periods.
FR-052 MUST: Report realised-vs-projected variance and recompute indicative remaining payback using the same accounting treatment as F08.
FR-053 MUST: Identify potential underperformance reasons such as lower generation, consumption shifts, settlement treatment or changed maintenance/financing costs.
FR-054 MUST: Label verified explanations separately from unverified possibilities and never silently substitute new assumptions for the original projection.

4. BUSINESS / CALCULATION RULES
BR-01 No official credit is issued, changed or transferred by Solaris; DISCOM approval and settlement remain external.
BR-02 'Household maximum' is an input ceiling; accepted commitment is separately recorded under F06.
BR-03 Valid plan: funding balance, relevant constraints, valid shares, stated objective/solver status, and approved share treatment only when independently supported.
BR-04 Projected credit kWh != generation kWh; monetary bill savings depend on applicable credit/tariff rules and usage/settlement treatment.
BR-05 Year-end or carry-forward credits are not assumed to be worthless; their value and timing depend on the applicable rule set.
BR-06 No credit comparison between unmatched periods is automatically a DISCOM error.
BR-07 Official 'recorded credits' require source bill/settlement evidence; a synthetic example is labelled synthetic.
BR-08 No resident/project is treated as legally approved based only on a Solaris checklist.
BR-09 A net utility bill amount must not have an applied solar credit subtracted again.
BR-10 For finance reporting, treat upfront contributions, loan principal, interest, ongoing payments and maintenance consistently; do not double-count paid principal as an extra project cost.
BR-11 Reports must distinguish energy units (kWh), currency (INR), percentages and dates/time zones.

5. NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
NFR-01 Privacy: least-privilege and project/household isolation; explicit consent and masked meter/account IDs; no public sample of actual consumer data.
NFR-02 Integrity: immutable or versioned source documents, projection assumptions, member acceptance, ownership shares, approvals and settlement imports.
NFR-03 Security: server-side permission checks, secure sessions, HTTPS on deployed environment, restricted document access, upload MIME/size checking and basic abuse controls.
NFR-04 Arithmetic: deterministic currency/energy calculations, exact rounding policy, unit tests for totals, negative/zero values and tariff assumptions.
NFR-05 Explainability: figures reveal source, effective period, model rule, assumption and whether estimated or recorded.
NFR-06 Accessibility: mobile-responsive, readable contrast, keyboard navigation and explicit labels for estimated/official information.
NFR-07 Performance: 20-household scenario should complete within an explicit measured hackathon-demo budget; timeouts return a truthful status.
NFR-08 Resilience: no dependence on live DISCOM/inverter endpoints; documented synthetic/manual/CSV fallback for demonstration.

6. ACCEPTANCE TESTS
AC-01 Two residents cannot access each other's full bills or private contribution limits; provider cannot see either without permission.
AC-02 Resident can save manual kWh/budget without a PDF or optional bill amount; OCR upload requires confirmation before use.
AC-03 VNM check without verified supporting material says further verification required, not 'approved'.
AC-04 Equal, affordability-aware and benefit-aware plans have explainable differences, constrained share totals and valid project funding or clearly reported infeasibility.
AC-05 Withdrawal or lower generation modifies a new what-if result without changing accepted baseline records.
AC-06 Provider quote update flags F04/F05 projections based on the previous quote as stale.
AC-07 Post-installation OCR user correction persists and drives F09 comparison.
AC-08 Missing settlement evidence blocks a definitive expected-credit claim; mismatched periods remain pending alignment.
AC-09 Credits carried forward are not reported as lost just because unused this month.
AC-10 Financial dashboard avoids the credit/principal double-counting traps and accurately records expense timing.
AC-11 F10 shows both original forecast and actual outcomes with justified explanations, not an overwritten projection.
AC-12 Every exported financial statement is labelled as an internal report, not a utility bill.

7. EXCLUSIONS FROM HACKCBS BUILD
No MSME-only workflow, technician ticketing, payment processing, loan origination, voting, contract execution, live automatic official settlement feed, inverter API, P2P energy trading, transfer of solar credits, or guaranteed financial returns.
