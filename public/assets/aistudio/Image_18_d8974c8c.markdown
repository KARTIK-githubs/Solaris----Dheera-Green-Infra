## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an India-first climate-tech identity rooted in community, clarity, and institutional reliability. Drawing aesthetic discipline from precision engineering tools and clean-energy financial analytics, the visual language combines the meticulous structure of modern developer platforms with the executive poise of enterprise fintech.

The interface serves residential cluster leaders, institutional energy managers, and commercial cooperative planners. Every visual touchpoint must balance infrastructural authority with high-velocity utility. 

Key attributes:
- **Atmospheric Precision:** Crisp metric hierarchies, razor-thin framing, and mathematically disciplined alignments that communicate computational certainty.
- **Ecological Optimism:** Deep forest and sunlit metallic accents replace typical stark neon utility palettes, anchoring solar power in natural vitality and tangible financial yield.
- **Architectural Density:** Information-dense yet legible layouts tailored for complex geographic simulations, solar irradiation maps, and multi-tenant tariff distribution models.

## Colors

The palette grounds high-tech calculations in deep earth tones, utilizing solar wavelengths for actionable emphasis.

### Palette Architecture
- **Brand Primary (`#103D35`):** Deep Solar Forest Green. Anchors primary structural frames, dark navigation bars, high-prominence headers, and high-impact stat containers.
- **Action Emerald (`#157B65`):** Vibrant operative green. Governs primary call-to-actions, active toggle segments, confirmed yield charts, and positive delta indicators.
- **Solar Gold (`#F5BD4F`):** Radiant sunlight indicator. Reserved for peak kilowatt-hour (kWh) metrics, photodiode sensor status indicators, irradiance hotspots, and spotlighted community milestones.
- **Canvas Base (`#F8FAF7`):** Off-white base canvas preventing optical fatigue during intense financial modelling and GIS rooftop analysis.
- **Surface Layer (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity elevated planes with microscopic boundary contrast.
- **Subtle Tint (`#E7F5EE`):** Soft Mint. Used for container backgrounds of approved statuses, energy credits, and subtle positive table rows.
- **Text Systems:**
  - Primary Content: `#1C2930` (Dark Charcoal) at high contrast for optimal legibility across varied outdoor lighting conditions.
  - Secondary Metadata & Units: `#687A80` (Slate Grey) for electrical units (kWp, kWh, MWh), timestamps, and grid specifications.
- **System Feedback:**
  - Warning/Regulatory: `#B8791B` (Warning Amber) for grid tariff shifts and net-metering capping thresholds.
  - Infeasibility/Critical: `#D75A55` (Coral Red) for structural rooftop overload, shadow occlusion warnings, or system fault telemetry.

## Typography

The dual-type setup enforces a rigorous distinction between high-impact structural metrics and quantitative tabular analytics.

- **Headlines & Primary Metrics (`Manrope`):** Delivers clean geometry, crisp apexes, and balanced circular forms. Used for major project headers, dashboard metric readouts, and financial yield summaries. Always set metrics with tighter tracking to produce an engineered, instrumentation-grade feel.
- **Interface & Data Engine (`Inter`):** Selected for high legibility, robust screen rendering across density ratios, and tabular numeral support (`tnum`). All solar yields, monthly generation trends, Rupee values, kilowatt-hour meters, and microgrid logs must use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to eliminate spatial jitter during active polling cycles.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a strict **8px baseline spacing system** (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 32px), creating vertical and horizontal predictability across complex data density tiers.

### Grid Foundations
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with 24px gutters and 32px canvas margins. Centered within an expansive max-width container of 1440px for analytical cockpits.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with 16px gutters and 24px margins. Dual-column data structures collapse into single focal streams.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with 16px gutters and 16px outer safety margins.

### Composition Guidelines
- Metric tiles align in symmetric grid groups with horizontal `space-md` gaps.
- Complex community modeling views reserve a persistent 64px left rail or top-level compact command bar to retain vertical viewport capacity for topological map layers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **tonal layer transitions and hairline border definition** over aggressive drop shadows, preserving an analytical, map-first environment.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **Canvas Level (Ground 0):** `#F8FAF7`. Holds base navigation frames, outer canvas margins, and ambient structural backgrounds.
2. **Elevated Panels (Level 1):** `#FFFFFF`. The primary operational plane for cards, data grids, and rooftop diagnostic displays. Surrounded by a hairline border: `1px solid #E5EAE7`.
3. **Floating Overlays (Level 2):** `#FFFFFF`. Modals, popovers, drop-down menus, and context actions. Defined by `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(16, 61, 53, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(16, 61, 53, 0.04)` and framed by `1px solid #DCE3E0`.
4. **Hero Data Tiles (Inverted High Elevation):** `#103D35`. Deep forest blocks used sparingly for overall community generation totals, lifetime offset aggregators, and critical administrative controls.

Avoid standard black or neutral grey shadows; cast all elevation blurs with a faint primary-tinted footprint (`rgba(16, 61, 53, ...)`) to maintain a cohesive environment.

## Shapes

The platform employs a calibrated geometric curvature (`roundedness: 2`) that pairs soft, ergonomic edges with tight technical cohesion:

- **Core Cards & Modules:** `rounded-lg` (16px to 20px) creates a polished, high-end consumer tech silhouette for large analytics boxes, rooftop canvas containers, and group solar pool summaries.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Buttons, Dropdowns):** 8px (`rounded-md` / 0.5rem), providing a tactile, disciplined interface that balances precision with touch target clarity.
- **Pills & Status Indicators:** Full-radius (`9999px`), delivering clear contrast against square charts and metric cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#157B65`, foreground `#FFFFFF`, border-radius 8px. Hover state shifts to `#103D35` with a smooth 150ms transition. Active state applies `scale(0.99)`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with hairline border `1px solid #E5EAE7`, text `#1C2930`. Hover applies a `#E7F5EE` surface tint with `#157B65` text.
- **Solar High-Yield Action:** Background `#F5BD4F`, text `#103D35`, bold weight. Used strictly for "Instant Rooftop Quote" or "Commit Power Share".

### Status Badges & Pills
Constructed with full roundedness (`9999px`), `0.6875rem` uppercase bold font, tracking `0.04em`, and 4px vertical / 10px horizontal padding.
- **Estimated:** Background `#F1F3F2`, text `#687A80`, border `1px solid #E2E6E4`.
- **Simulated:** Background `#FFF7E6`, text `#B8791B`, border `1px solid #FCE4B8`.
- **Submitted:** Background `#EBF3FC`, text `#2A60A4`, border `1px solid #D5E5F9`.
- **Approved / Verified:** Background `#E7F5EE`, text `#157B65`, border `1px solid #C4E7D5`.
- **Infeasible / Rejected:** Background `#FDEEED`, text `#D75A55`, border `1px solid #F9CBC9`.

### Metric Counters
Dedicated analytical displays pairing a crisp `label-sm` upper text in `#687A80` with an expansive `metric-md` or `metric-xl` figure in `#1C2930`. Directly adjacent units (`kWh`, `kWp`, `INR/₹`) sit in `Inter` medium text at baseline alignment, scaled to 50% of the metric font size.

### Segmented Controls
Enclosed pill-toggle containers with a `#F1F4F2` background, 6px interior padding, and 8px outer radius. The active segment presents a pure `#FFFFFF` elevated tile with a micro-shadow (`0 1px 3px rgba(16, 61, 53, 0.08)`) and deep `#103D35` typography.

### Input Fields
Standard height 40px, border-radius 8px, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E5EAE7`. On focus, transitions cleanly to a `1.5px solid #157B65` border with an ambient emerald glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(21, 123, 101, 0.12)`. Tabular unit adornments (such as "Sq. Ft." or "kW") nest inside the right edge in `#687A80`.

### Cards & Analytical Containers
Drawn with a pure white canvas (`#FFFFFF`), `rounded-lg` (16px), and bordered by a razor-thin line (`1px solid #E5EAE7`). Internal elements are padded with `space-lg` (24px). Headers feature an integrated bottom divider (`1px solid #F8FAF7`) with action slots on the right.
